# Task 4 - Severity & Case Classification

## Objective
To classify adverse drug reactions based on seriousness and expectedness according to pharmacovigilance principles.

---

## Case 01

**Drug:** Amoxicillin

**Adverse Reaction:** Skin rash and itching

**Seriousness:** Non-serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Assessment:** The reaction is a common type of hypersensitivity/adverse reaction associated with amoxicillin. It does not indicate a serious outcome based on the information provided.

---

## Case 02

**Drug:** Ibuprofen

**Adverse Reaction:** Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization

**Seriousness:** Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Assessment:** The event is serious because it required hospitalization. Gastrointestinal bleeding is a recognized adverse reaction associated with NSAIDs such as ibuprofen.

---

## Case 03

**Drug:** Metformin

**Adverse Reaction:** Lactic acidosis

**Seriousness:** Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Assessment:** Lactic acidosis is a serious and potentially life-threatening adverse reaction associated with metformin. The case is therefore classified as serious.

---

## Summary

| Case | Drug | Adverse Reaction | Seriousness | Expectedness |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|--------------|
| 01 | Amoxicillin | Skin rash, itching | Non-serious | Expected |
| 02 | Ibuprofen | Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization | Serious | Expected |
| 03 | Metformin | Lactic acidosis | Serious | Expected |

## Conclusion
Cases were classified according to the seriousness of the adverse reaction and whether the reaction is a recognized adverse effect of the suspected drug.
